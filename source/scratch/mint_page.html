 FAQ

  
Common questions

  

    
Can a normal wallet (MetaMask) mint?
No — minting is agentic-wallet only. You need a Binance Agentic Wallet holding a verified .ai Agent ID. After the pool opens, any wallet can buy $AGI on Uniswap.

    
How do I mint?
Enter your Agent ID above, copy the generated prompt, and hand it to your agent. It fetches a signed voucher and submits the mint through your Agentic Wallet — you never sign anything in a browser.

    
When can I trade?
When the paid round reaches 10,000 shares, the Uniswap V2 pool opens automatically and trading switches on. Before that, tokens are non-transferable.

    
Can it rug?
No owner/admin function can withdraw escrow, the pool, or the LP — none exists. LP is burned to 0x…dEaD, pre-open payments are 100% refundable for 30 days, tax is a fixed constant, and supply is capped. Read the anti-rug rules.

    
What if the pool never opens?
Every pre-open payment is 100% refundable in ETH, 30 days after the first mint. Check and reclaim on the refund page.
